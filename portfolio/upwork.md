# AskDock: Upwork portfolio entry

## Project title (65 / 70 characters)

AskDock – AI Chatbot SaaS with RAG, Lead Capture & Stripe Billing

## My role

Product designer & full-stack developer: UX, visual design, front end, back end, AI pipeline and deployment (concept project).

## Description (480 / 600 characters)

AskDock is a SaaS that turns a small business's PDFs, Word docs and website into an AI chatbot that installs with one script tag.
• RAG chat with streamed, cited answers
• PDF/DOCX upload and website crawling into pgvector
• Brand customiser with live preview + embeddable widget
• Lead capture when the bot can't answer
• Analytics, knowledge gaps and leads pipeline
• Stripe subscriptions with enforced plan limits
Next.js 15, Supabase, OpenAI, Stripe. All content is demo data.
